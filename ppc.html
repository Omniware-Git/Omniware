What We Offer

Amazon Ads Management

Expert management of your Amazon advertising campaigns. We optimise your ad spend, improve targeting, and maximise your return on investment to help your products reach the right customers.

Automation Software

Powerful automation tools designed to streamline your Amazon advertising. Our software handles bid adjustments, keyword optimisation, and campaign management, saving you time while improving performance.

Intelligent keyword discovery and ASIN targeting workflows. Identify converting search terms, manage your keyword pipeline, and ensure your campaigns are always targeting the right audiences.

Part of the Omniware Platform

PPC Management is a module within the Omniware platform, available alongside our reporting and profit tracking tools. Manage your advertising alongside the rest of your Amazon business in one place.

The PPC module connects directly to your Amazon Advertising account via the Amazon Ads API, giving you real-time data and automated campaign management without leaving the platform.
